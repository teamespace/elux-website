## The Challenge

Travelcircus already offered a broad range of travel packages across city breaks, theme parks, concerts, wellness, and ticketed experiences.

The challenge was to bring that feature-rich web experience into a mobile app without making discovery, booking, and ticket management feel crowded or fragmented.

## The Approach

We first mapped the existing web experience into a clear mobile product structure, defining the key user flows before moving into interface design.

- Structured the core journeys across discovery, offer details, booking, and ticketing.
- Translated the existing feature set into mobile-first interactions instead of shrinking desktop layouts.
- Reworked package details so users could understand what is included, pricing, and booking options more easily on smaller screens.
- Used interactive prototypes early to test complete journeys before locking the final direction.

## Prototype Early, Decide Faster

AI-assisted prototyping helped us test complete product flows earlier, not just individual screens.

The team could experience how discovery, offer details, booking, and ticketing worked together, making iteration faster and decisions easier to validate.
