## The Challenge

Alia helps Shopify brands grow subscribers and revenue through smarter popups, targeting, testing, personalization, and analytics.

The challenge was to explain that product depth clearly across the full website, from homepage to feature and detail pages, without making the experience feel heavy or fragmented.

## The Approach

We rebuilt the website around how potential customers understand and evaluate the product.

- Restructured the messaging so Alia's value, features, and proof were easier to scan.
- Defined a clearer UX and content hierarchy across homepage, inner pages, and product detail pages.
- Designed conversion paths that guide visitors from product understanding to deeper exploration and action.
- Built the final experience in Webflow so the design, interaction, and responsive behavior stayed consistent across the full site.

## The Website as Part of the Product Journey

For a conversion-focused SaaS, the website is part of how the product is understood and evaluated.

We treated each page as one connected experience, helping visitors move from understanding Alia's value to exploring the product and taking the next step.
