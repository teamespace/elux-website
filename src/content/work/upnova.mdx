## The Challenge

Upnova needed more than a visual refresh. As a Shopify upsell platform focused on increasing AOV and conversion, the website had to explain the product more clearly, strengthen its positioning, improve SEO structure, and guide visitors toward action.

## The Approach

We rebuilt the website around a clearer product story and a more conversion-focused experience.

- Restructured the content around the product, its use cases, and the value it creates for Shopify brands.
- Designed the website to support clearer navigation, stronger SEO foundations, and more direct conversion paths.
- Used working experiences early in the process so decisions could be made from how the product felt in use, not only from static screens.

## Faster Iteration, Better Decisions

AI helped us shorten the iteration loop. Working experiences gave the team something real to review earlier, making it easier to evaluate the direction, test how the flow worked, and move decisions forward faster.

That same collaboration later extended beyond the website into Upnova's Shopify dashboard, where we supported product enhancements and new AI-powered features.
