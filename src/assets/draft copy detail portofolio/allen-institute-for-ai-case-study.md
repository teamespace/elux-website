## THE CHALLENGE

Allen Institute for AI works across advanced AI research, open models, and technical tools. The challenge was to turn that complexity into product experiences that felt clear without oversimplifying the depth behind the work.

Across the dashboard and model website, users needed to understand what the product does, how to explore it, and where to go next.

## THE APPROACH

We structured the experience around clarity first, then shaped the interface around how users would actually explore the product.

- Simplified complex technical information into clearer product flows and content hierarchy.
- Designed the dashboard around easier navigation, scanning, and access to key information.
- Shaped the model website around stronger product storytelling, technical structure, and open model access.
- Used AI-assisted iteration to explore directions faster while keeping product decisions grounded in usability and context.

## CLARITY WITHOUT LOSING THE DEPTH

The goal was never to make the product feel simpler than it is. It was to make the complexity easier to navigate.

AI-assisted iteration helped us test structure and interface directions faster, while the final decisions stayed focused on how researchers, developers, and technical users would understand and explore the product.
