## THE CHALLENGE

Saifa AI brings several connected capabilities into one product, including omnichannel chat, chat with memory, data sources, integrations, and analytics.

The challenge was not only to create a recognizable brand, but also to structure those capabilities into a product experience that felt clear, connected, and easy to navigate as the platform grew.

## THE APPROACH

We developed the brand and product experience together, starting from how users would move through the platform.

- Created the Saifa AI logo and visual identity to establish a distinct product presence.
- Defined the UX structure and information hierarchy around the platform’s core capabilities.
- Designed clear flows for Omnichannel Chat, Chat with Memory, Data Sources, Integrations, and Analytics.
- Translated the brand language into navigation, components, states, and dashboard interactions.
- Kept the system consistent so new features could extend without making the product feel fragmented.

## BRAND THAT WORKS INSIDE THE PRODUCT

The identity was designed to work beyond the logo. It became part of how the product communicates hierarchy, navigation, and interaction.

By connecting branding with UX and interface design, Saifa AI could feel like one product from first impression through daily use.
