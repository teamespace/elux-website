## THE CHALLENGE

Global Minang Ventura was preparing to expand its business across international markets and needed a website that could match that ambition. It had to clearly communicate the company, its business model, and the Sederhana brand without slowing down the launch.

## THE APPROACH

Instead of waiting for a complete Figma handoff before development, we moved from brief to working website early.

- Built the first working concept in two hours.
- Used the working website alongside Figma to refine the visual direction and overall experience.
- Moved the approved direction into production and launched within one week.

## WHY WE BUILT EARLY

A working website made decisions faster. The client could evaluate more than static screens and see how the content, interactions, and page flow worked together before locking the final direction.

AI helped shorten that loop, making it easier to test, refine, and ship without separating design and development into long handoff stages.
